About me

Hi 👋, I'm Yousif Ali

Welcome to my portfolio

I am an Automation and Control Engineer with a passion for building intelligent and reliable industrial systems. My experience spans PLC and SCADA development, Python-based backend integration, and SQL data management for real-time control and monitoring.

My journey began with a deep interest in motion control during my graduation project—developing a control system for a prosthetic foot testing machine. That project sparked my dedication to combining hardware, software, and data to create smarter automation systems.

Currently, I work at HA Technology, where I design, test, and optimize PLC/SCADA systems for ready-mix automation and solar PV monitoring projects. I have built Python + FastAPI backends that connect PLCs with MS SQL Server databases , enabling data historians, alarm management, and SCADA dashboards.

I am driven by the idea of integrating industrial automation with modern software technologies—turning raw machine data into actionable insights. My next goal is to deepen my expertise in SCADA platforms (like AVEVA, WinCC) and data-driven automation systems.
