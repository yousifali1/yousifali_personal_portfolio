About me

Hi 👋, I'm Yousif Ali

Welcome to my portfolio

I am an Automation and Control Engineer with a passion for building intelligent and reliable industrial systems. My experience spans PLC and SCADA development, Python-based backend integration, and SQL data management for real-time control and monitoring.

My journey began with a deep interest in motion control during my graduation project—developing a control system for a prosthetic foot testing machine. That project sparked my dedication to combining hardware, software, and data to create smarter automation systems.

Currently, I work as a Controls Engineer at Dematic through TEXVIA, an Ireland-based company, designing and implementing control system hardware and software for a large-scale Amazon fulfillment center. Alongside this, I work part-time as an Automation Engineer at TEXVIA, where we help industrial enterprises improve efficiency, achieve real-time visibility, and future-proof their operations through Industry 4.0 and digital transformation technologies.

I am driven by the idea of integrating industrial automation with modern software technologies—turning raw machine data into actionable insights. My next goal is to deepen my expertise in SCADA platforms (such as AVEVA and WinCC) and data-driven automation systems.
